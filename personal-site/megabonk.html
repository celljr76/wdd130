Megabonk is an indie game created by the indie creator Vedinad. The game was inspired by Vampire Survivors. Indie games (short for independent video game) are essentially games created by smaller development teams or individuals without technical and financial support. Celljr76 first saw Megabonk being played by one of his favorite youtubers to watch "CaptianSparklez". He wanted to give it a try, and thought he could do better than his favorite youtuber to watch. He was wrong. The game can be difficult to start out, but as you unlock certain items, weapons, and tomes for the game it becomes easier. You can also get unlocks by playing the game more as well. The unlocks gives you the ability to get more tomes and weapons for each run you do. When you start out the game you are given 2 characters to choose from to play, "The Fox" and "Sir Oofie". Each character has a unique weapon to them when you play them. There are other characters to unlock, up to 20 to unlock doing certain challenges or special conditions for them. When you start out you can have up to 2 weapons and 2 tomes, and when you get further in the game you can unlock to be able to have up to 4 weapons and 4 tomes. There are currently two maps Desert and Forest. Forest is a little easier to start playing out on, because Desert you get sandstorms as the environment every often, whcih makes it hard to the enemies around you and where you are going. This is a good game if you to challenge yourself with surviving and unlocking certain aspecs of the game as you go on.
