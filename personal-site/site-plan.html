Overview

Purpose

My personal website will be an informational website about video games, and specific games that I am interested in. I want it to be a website where gamers can add their input into the games that I like and play when I have time. It will talk about my opinions of the game, how long I've played the game for, and pictures of in game content. I want it to be a community type page like the water rafting website. Where all likes of people can enjoy whether they are new to gaming or have been a gamer for a long time.

Audience

My audience is to target people who are new to gaming or have been gamers for a while, and the in between.

MegaBonk

Megabonk is an indie game created by indie creator Vedinad. The game was inspired by Vampire Survivors. Indie games (short for independent video game) are essentially games created by smaller development teams or individuals without technical and financial support. Celljr76 first saw Megabonk being played by one of his favorite youtubers to watch "CaptainSparklez". He wanted to give it a try, and thought he could do better than his favorite youtuber to watch. He was wrong. The game can be difficult to start out, but as you unlock certain items, weapons, and tomes for the game it becomes easier. You can also get unlocks by playing the game more as well. The unlocks gives you the ability to get more tomes and weapons for each run you do. When you start out in the game you are given 2 characters to choose from to play, "The Fox" and "Sir Oofie". Each character has a unique weapon to them when you play them. There are other characters to unlock, up to 20 to unlock doing certain challenges or special conditions for them. When you start out you can have up to 2 weapons and 2 tomes, and when you get further in the game you can unlock to be able to have up to 4 weapons and 4 tomes. There are currently two maps Desert and Forest. Forest is a little easier to start playing out on, because with Desert you get sandstorms as the environment every so often, which makes it hard to see the enemies around you and where you are going. This is a good game if you like to challenge yourself with surviving and unlocking certain aspects of the game as you go on.

Rocket League

Rocket League is an interesting game. It was created by the company Psyonix and was first released in July 2015. It's a game where you play vehicular soccer. You can play different modes in the game. There are also many different cars you can choose from to play. Currently celljr76 is playing with the car fennec. He usually plays with his friends, but not by himself. You can customize the look of your car, ranging from the color and decal on your car to the sound of the engine. You can also have a hat and an antenna. There is boost to help you fly for a bit, and you can customize its effects. You can also have an effect appearing when you make a goal. There is a rumble mode where you can gain certain items every 10 seconds which makes the gameplay interesting and fun. The regular modes can range from one on one to 4 on 4. There is also a competitive mode to it, where you can get ranks ranging from bronze to SSL. Celljr76 is not a very competitive player when it comes to this game but just plays it for fun, and to have a good time with friends.
