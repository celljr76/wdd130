Rocket League is an interesting game. It was created by the company Psyonix and was first released in July 2015. It's a game where you play vehicular soccer. You can play different modes in the game. There are also many different cars you can choose from to play. Currently celljr76 is playing with the car fennec. He usually plays with his friends, but not by himself. You can customize the look of your car, ranging from the color or decal on your car to the sound of the engine. You can also have a hat and an antenna. There is boost to help you fly for a bit, and you customize its effects. You can also have an effect appearing when you make a goal. There is a rumble mode where you can gain certain powerups every 10 seconds which makes the gameplay interesting and fun. The regular modes can range from one on one to 4 on 4. There is also a competitive mode to it, where you can get ranks ranging from bronze to SSL. Celljr76 is not a very competitive player when it comes to this game but just plays it for fun, and to have a good time with friends.
